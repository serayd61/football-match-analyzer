### en-US (varsayılan)
**Short description (76):**
Football match probabilities from a statistical model, every prediction scored.

**Full description:**
Football Analytics Pro publishes match probabilities for 11 leagues from a Dixon-Coles statistical model – and keeps score in public.

WHAT YOU GET
• 1X2, over/under, both-teams-to-score and expected-goals probabilities before kick-off
• Likely scorelines, Asian handicap lines and a model-vs-market "value radar"
• A public performance page: hit rate, calibration and returns, settled after every match – wins and losses alike
• League tables, form, absences and head-to-head context for every fixture
• Follow your teams and leagues; get the day's predictions in one view

LEAGUES
Premier League, Championship, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Liga Portugal, Champions League, Brasileirão, Süper Lig.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Model – a Dixon-Coles bivariate Poisson model with xG, Elo and isotonic calibration, blended with market odds.
2. Predict – every prediction is time-stamped before kick-off and cannot be edited afterwards.
3. Settle – after the final whistle the prediction is scored and published on the performance page.

PLANS
A free plan shows today's headline probabilities. The Pro subscription (7-day free trial, cancel anytime) unlocks all markets, likely scores, the value radar and the full archive.

IMPORTANT
Football Analytics Pro is an information and statistics service. It does not offer betting, does not accept stakes and is not affiliated with any bookmaker. Probabilities are statistical estimates, not financial advice. 18+. Please play responsibly.
