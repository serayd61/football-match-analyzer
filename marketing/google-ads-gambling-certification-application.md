### Business description (paste into the form)

footballanalytics.pro is an independent football statistics platform operated by SwissDigital Consulting (Dottikon, Switzerland). The service publishes match probabilities (home / draw / away, expected goals, over/under) produced by a Dixon-Coles statistical model with calibration and walk-forward backtesting. Every prediction is time-stamped before kick-off, settled after the match and published on a public performance page, so users can verify the model's historical accuracy.

The site does not offer gambling of any kind. It does not accept bets or stakes, does not hold customer funds, does not process gambling payments and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or paid by any bookmaker or gambling operator. Revenue comes solely from a monthly subscription (7-day free trial) that gives access to the model's outputs and analysis tools.

The website contains no outbound links to gambling operators. Should such links be added in the future, they will exclusively point to operators licensed and authorised in the user's country, in line with Google's gambling-related information policy.

Responsible-gambling measures on the site: an 18+ notice and "play responsibly" line in the footer of every page; a compliance statement confirming that no betting is offered; responsible-gambling helplines for Switzerland (sos-spielsucht.ch, 0800 040 080), Germany (check-dein-spiel.de, 0800 137 27 00), Austria (spielsuchthilfe.at) and an international resource (gamblingtherapy.org); and a disclaimer that predictions are statistical estimates and not financial advice.

We request certification for Switzerland under the "gambling-related information" category so that we can run Search and Performance Max campaigns for the subscription product. Ads will target adults only (18+) and will not include bonus, free-bet or promotional gambling offers.
